# Human scoring

Two reviewers independently rated anonymous A/B answers. Ratings were assigned per question and system.

- Correctness: 0 = incorrect; 1 = partly correct; 2 = correctly distinguishes the source-supported claim from distractors.
- Evidence support: 0 = unsupported; 1 = indirect or incomplete support; 2 = direct support.
- Preference: A, B, or Tie.

Reviewer scores were averaged within each question and system. Paired bootstrap and sign-flip tests used questions as the unit of analysis. Agreement was measured using exact agreement and Cohen’s kappa.
Citation validity and hallucination were not separately rated. Automatic PMID occurrence measures citation presence only.
